# Piano: the Salamander Grand Piano

**What:** 32 notes of the **Salamander Grand Piano**, a Yamaha C5 recorded by Alexander Holm.

- 16 pitches every minor third from A1 to F♯5 (`A1` … `Fs5`).
- Two of its 16 loudness levels: `v5`, soft, for the left hand; `v9`, fuller, for the right hand.

The site credits it on How it works: "Piano: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (CC BY 3.0)".

**Source:** the MP3 edition made for the web by Tone.js's piano, downloaded on 23 November 2026:

- address: `https://tambien.github.io/Piano/audio/<note>v<level>.mp3`;
- format: 128 kbps, 44.1 kHz, stereo, unchanged;
- size: 7.1 MB for the 32 files.

**Use:** the site's music (`music.js`), in its Piano and Concerto sounds, decodes them once the music is turned on, folds each to mono, trims it and plays every note from its nearest sample, retuned by at most a semitone and a half.
