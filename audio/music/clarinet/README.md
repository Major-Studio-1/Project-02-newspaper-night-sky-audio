# Clarinet: VSCO 2 Community Edition

**What:** six long, sustained notes of a B♭ clarinet at medium loudness (`v2` of 3), from the **VSCO 2 Community Edition** orchestra (Versilian Studios): `Woodwinds/Clarinet/susLong`.

**Licence:** [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE/blob/master/LICENSE), public domain. No credit is required; the site names it on How it works all the same.

**Source:** `https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE`, downloaded on 23 November 2026: six WAV files, 44.1 kHz, stereo.

**Pitches:**

- The original files name their octaves one lower than scientific pitch: `D3` sounds D4 (294 Hz).
- Measured by autocorrelation, they sound A♯3, D4, F4, A♯4, D5, F5, 1 to 6 cents sharp.
- They are kept here under their sounding names (`As3.mp3` … `F5.mp3`).

**Format here:** each file cut from its first sound to its last and saved as 128 kbps MP3; 152 to 186 KB each, about 1 MB in all.

**Use:** the site's music (`music.js`), in its Concerto sound only, decodes them once Concerto is chosen, folds each to mono and plays every note from its nearest sample, retuned by at most two semitones.
